Please read the statement carefully.</span></p><p>Yukari Yakumo has placed $$$x$$$ boundary stones of power $$$1$$$ and $$$y$$$ boundary stones of power $$$2$$$ on the ground.</p><p>Yukari has a spell $$$s$$$ consisting of the characters <span class="tex-font-style-tt">+</span> and <span class="tex-font-style-tt">-</span> ($$$|s| = m$$$). Yukari chooses a prefix of $$$s$$$ (possibly empty) and then performs every character of this prefix in order.</p><p>When performing the $$$i$$$-th character of the prefix, Yukari chooses a stone that is currently on the ground. The chosen stone changes as follows: </p><ul> <li> if $$$s_i = \mathtt{+}$$$, its power increases by $$$1$$$; however, if its power is $$$2$$$, then its power does not change, and a new stone of power $$$2$$$ appears on the ground instead (the original stone stays on the ground); </li><li> if $$$s_i = \mathtt{-}$$$, its power decreases by $$$1$$$; however, if its power is $$$1$$$, then the stone disappears. </li></ul><p>If there is no stone on the ground, the remaining characters cannot be performed, and the number of stones on the ground at that moment is $$$0$$$.</p><p>Yukari chooses the prefix and the stone for every spell so that the number of stones on the ground is as large as possible. Find this maximum number of stones.</p></div><div class="input-specification"><div class="section-title">Input</div><p>Each test contains multiple test cases. The first line contains the number of test cases $$$t$$$ ($$$1 \le t \le 100$$$).
